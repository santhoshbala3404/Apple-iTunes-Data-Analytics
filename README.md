# Apple iTunes Data Analytics

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Apple iTunes Music Store data to understand customer purchasing behavior, revenue trends, product performance, and business operations.

Using SQL and Power BI, the project explores customer transactions, invoice details, track sales, playlist performance, employee contributions, and geographic revenue distribution.

The objective is to identify meaningful business insights that can support revenue optimization, customer retention, and strategic decision-making.

## Tools & Technologies
- **SQL:** Data querying, joins, aggregations, and advanced analysis.
- **Microsoft Power BI:** Interactive dashboard development and data visualization.

## Key Areas of Analysis

### 1. Customer Analysis
- Customer spending patterns.
- Average customer lifetime spending.
- One-time versus repeat purchasers.
- Revenue generated per customer across countries.

### 2. Sales and Revenue Analysis
- Monthly revenue trends.
- Average invoice value.
- Monthly and quarterly sales performance.
- Employee-wise revenue contribution.

### 3. Product and Content Analysis
- Top-performing tracks.
- Popular playlist categories.
- Track sales performance.
- Tracks available in playlists but not appearing in invoice sales.

### 4. Employee and Operational Analysis
- Employee contribution to revenue.
- Customer spending by assigned sales representative.
- Average customer allocation per employee.
- Employee-wise geographic revenue contribution.

### 5. Geographic Analysis
- Customer distribution by country and city.
- Revenue generated across different countries.
- Revenue contribution by billing location.
- Revenue per customer across geographic regions.

### 6. Customer Retention Analysis
- Customer purchase frequency.
- Repeat purchase patterns.
- Average time between purchases.
- Customer engagement trends.

## Key Insights
- All 59 customers in the analyzed dataset are repeat purchasers.
- August 2019 recorded the highest monthly revenue at approximately 159.39.
- October 2019 and January 2020 recorded the lowest monthly revenue at approximately 43.56.
- March recorded the highest monthly sales volume.
- Q1 (January–March) was the strongest-performing quarter.
- Q4 (October–December) was the lowest-performing quarter.
- The generic Music playlist recorded the highest quantity sold, followed by 90's Music.

## Project Files
- `apple_itunes_da_project.sql` – SQL queries for business analysis.
- `apple_itunes_data_analysis.pbix` – Power BI dashboard.
- `apple_itunes_data_analysis_insights.pdf` – Detailed analysis report.

## Business Recommendations
- Focus on customer acquisition while maintaining strong customer retention.
- Investigate revenue fluctuations during lower-performing months.
- Develop targeted campaigns during weaker sales periods.
- Explore opportunities to promote niche playlist categories.
- Analyze geographic performance to identify growth opportunities.
- Use employee performance insights to improve operational efficiency.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how SQL and Power BI can transform transactional data into meaningful business insights. The analysis provides a structured understanding of customer behavior, sales performance, geographic trends, and operational efficiency.
